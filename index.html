Common Questions

Before You Request an Estimate

What information should I send for pricing?
The city, approximate square footage, rooms, existing flooring, desired flooring type, stairs and your preferred timeline are the most helpful starting points.
Do you handle floor preparation?
Yes, when included in the project scope. Preparation may include removal, grinding, repairs or other work required before installation.
Do you work on renovations and new construction?
Yes. CHIVV Flooring works on residential renovation projects and flooring scopes for construction projects across Metro Atlanta.

Hardwood, Engineered Flooring & Refinishing Across North Atlanta

CHIVV Flooring serves homeowners and projects across Johns Creek, Alpharetta, Sandy Springs, Buckhead, Roswell, Milton, Brookhaven, Dunwoody, East Cobb, Marietta, Cumming and the greater Metro Atlanta area. Project scopes can include hardwood installation and refinishing, engineered hardwood, LVP and laminate, stairs and trim, flooring removal and preparation, tile, bathrooms and showers.

Each project is evaluated around the actual flooring product, substrate and site conditions. That helps customers understand the work required before installation begins and helps keep the estimate aligned with the real project.
