Common Questions

Before You Request an Estimate

What information should I send for pricing?

The city, approximate square footage, rooms, existing flooring, desired flooring type, stairs and your preferred timeline are the most helpful starting points.

Do you handle floor preparation?

Yes, when included in the project scope. Preparation may include removal, grinding, repairs or other work required before installation.

Do you work on renovations and new construction?

Yes. CHIVV Flooring works on residential renovation projects and flooring scopes for construction projects across Metro Atlanta.

Metro Atlanta Flooring Contractor

Hardwood, Engineered Flooring & Refinishing Across North Atlanta

CHIVV Flooring serves homeowners, remodelers and construction projects across Alpharetta, Johns Creek, Roswell, Milton, Cumming, Sandy Springs, Buckhead in Atlanta, Marietta, Kennesaw and the greater Metro Atlanta area. Our flooring scope can include hardwood installation, hardwood sanding and refinishing, engineered hardwood, LVP and laminate installation, stairs and trim, plus floor removal, grinding, repair and preparation when included in the estimate.

For tile, bathroom or shower work, describe the surfaces and layout changes you have in mind so the related work can be defined. Each project is evaluated around the actual materials and site conditions. That helps customers understand the work required before installation begins and helps keep the estimate aligned with the real project.
