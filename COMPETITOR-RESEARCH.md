## Live-review worksheet

For each high-intent query, an owner/marketer should capture the date, device, broad search location, ads/LSA/local-pack/organic composition, result URL and the following observations for the top legitimate providers:

1. service named in title and H1;
2. whether location is an eligible office or service area;
3. first-screen Call/Text/Estimate availability;
4. estimate friction and qualification fields;
5. content coverage (use case, methods, preparation, process, FAQs);
6. genuine project galleries and proof attribution;
7. review presentation linked to a live source;
8. mobile sticky actions and tap sizes;
9. page speed/layout stability;
10. internal links between services, areas and project evidence.

Use public pages only. Do not copy wording, imagery or claims.

## Actionable opportunities independent of rankings

- Keep one strong page per distinct service intent and connect it contextually to substantive area pages.
- Lead with Call, Text and Free In-Home Estimate rather than requiring menu exploration.
- Win on decision usefulness: substrate, removal, occupied-home planning, transitions, stairs, preparation and what to provide for an estimate.
- Add real, permissioned project proof with material, scope and broad city only when verified.
- Display reviews through live public sources; never hard-code a changing rating or imply unverified themes.
- Treat a gallery as a conversion bridge: each future verified case study should link to the matching service and estimate form.

## Future advertising landing-page opportunities

Do not add pages until ads/query evidence and owner scope justify them. Potential focused variants are hardwood installation/replacement, engineered glue-down, refinishing, and shower remodeling for Tier 1 geo campaigns. Build only genuinely differentiated decision content, not city-name swaps.
