# CHIVV organic lead funnel
Prepared September 7, 2026. Proposed launch plan; social accounts, Google settings and production have not been changed.

## Positioning and offer
Working niche: homeowners refreshing or replacing existing floors in Buckhead (Atlanta), Sandy Springs, Milton and Alpharetta.
Lead with hardwood refinishing and the decision "refinish or replace?" Keep hardwood installation and LVP as supporting paths. This is a strategic hypothesis, not a claim based on measured search volume or job margins. Reassess using qualified leads, profitability and actual capacity.
Offer: request a free flooring estimate, with optional photos to help discuss next steps. Photos do not replace an on-site assessment or establish a binding price.
Suggested headline: "Refresh or replace your floors with CHIVV Flooring."
Supporting copy: "Tell us about your flooring project in Buckhead, Sandy Springs, Milton, Alpharetta or the surrounding Metro Atlanta area."
Primary action: Request a free flooring estimate. Secondary action: Call 678-571-7028.

## Customer journey
Discovery on Google, Instagram or Facebook → relevant service page → real project proof and answers → estimate request or call → personal follow-up → site visit where needed → written estimate → booked work → honest review request.
Visitors who are undecided should be able to read useful guidance without supplying an email. Start with a refinish-versus-replace guide and a flooring estimate preparation checklist. Do not add a newsletter until there is a real owner, consent process and delivery system.

## Local search content
Strengthen the existing Alpharetta and Milton pages with real local work. Plan Buckhead and Sandy Springs pages after collecting distinct project evidence and useful location-specific details. Buckhead is an Atlanta neighborhood; do not invent a separate business location there.
Build one useful guide on deciding between refinishing and replacement, linked to the relevant service pages. Do not create dozens of near-identical city/service pages. Google's spam policies describe doorway abuse involving substantially similar pages for closely related searches.
Source: https://developers.google.com/search/docs/essentials/spam-policies
Business Profile work: verify the actual business identity, relevant category, services, hours and service area; add real photos; request honest reviews from customers without screening for satisfaction.

## Lead handling
Assign a person to check new requests during business hours. Proposed internal response target: within one business hour when staffed; do not advertise that promise until consistently achievable.
Stages: New → Contacted → Qualified → Visit scheduled → Estimate sent → Won / Lost.
Qualified = within service area, relevant flooring scope, reachable contact and a plausible project timeline. Browsing customers can still receive helpful guidance.
Respond using the requested contact method. If email is preferred but absent, use the provided phone to arrange the next step.
Suggested follow-up: initial reply, one check-in after two business days, and one final helpful follow-up the next week if unanswered. Stop if asked. Do not enroll an estimate lead into promotional messages automatically.
After completed work, ask neutrally for an honest review using the verified review link. Do not publish an invented link.

## Measurement
Record received leads in a private lead log: received date, customer contact, city, service, source, campaign, landing page, timeline, status, next follow-up date, estimate value and won value. Never store customer details in this public repository.
Weekly metrics: qualified leads by source/service/area; contact rate; estimate rate; booked jobs; booked value. Use delivered unique inquiries as the denominator for qualification rate.
Website measurement: service-page visits → contact visits → attempts → received leads. Phone clicks show intent, not answered calls. Reconcile against actual inquiries.
Technical changes in this branch retain tagged source across navigation in the same browser tab for 30 minutes, capture external referrer hostname, remove arbitrary query strings from the stored landing URL, and label submissions as attempts. Storage-disabled browsers fall back to current-page data. Untagged social traffic may lack a referrer, so optional self-reported source helps.
No GA4 ID is installed by this change. Data-layer events alone do not send data to Analytics. Configure analytics separately and validate consent/privacy disclosures appropriate to the deployed setup. Do not send names, email, phone, free text or photos to Analytics.
A delivered-lead event is still pending host-side confirmation; do not treat estimate_form_attempt as generate_lead or a successful conversion.
