Shower Remodeling in Metro Atlanta

Start your shower remodel with a clear description of what needs to change. CHIVV Flooring / Bento’s Group can review your request around the existing shower, proposed surfaces and the boundaries of the renovation.

Describe the current shower and your priorities

Share photos of the full shower, entry, floor and adjoining bathroom. Explain whether your priority is worn surfaces, a different appearance, a layout change or easier use. If there is a known leak or visible damage, mention it directly; new tile alone should not be treated as a diagnosis or repair plan.

A project that extends into the rest of the room belongs in a bathroom remodeling discussion.

Define more than the visible tile

The estimate conversation should cover removal, the underlying assembly, water-management details, tile, grout, edges and transitions. Discuss any niche, bench or enclosure as a separate scope item. Avoid assuming that fixture relocation or glass supply is included simply because the shower is being updated.

See tile installation for layout and surface-planning questions. Product-specific requirements and existing conditions should guide the agreed work.

Confirm trade and supply responsibilities

Clarify who handles fixtures, any plumbing changes, enclosure measurements and other connected work. Responsibilities for applicable approvals and specialized trades need to be established before scheduling.

If you have already ordered tile or fixtures, share the product details and expected availability. A reference image is helpful, but it does not replace measurements or specifications.

Help us plan around your household

Tell us whether another shower is available and identify any access or scheduling constraints. Send your city, approximate shower dimensions, current photos, desired changes and timeframe. If you are unsure about the right scope, describe the problem and the outcome you want rather than choosing a solution in advance.

Project questions

Can I keep the existing shower layout?

Tell us that preference when requesting an estimate. Whether existing elements can remain depends on their condition and compatibility with the agreed renovation.

Can you price a shower from photos?

Photos help explain the project and guide follow-up questions. Measurements, existing conditions and selected components still need review before the full scope and price are established.
