Bathroom Remodeling in Metro Atlanta

Tell CHIVV Flooring / Bento’s Group what you want to change about your bathroom. A focused refresh and a larger renovation need different plans, so start with your priorities, existing layout and preferred timeframe.

Choose the changes that matter most

List what you want to keep as well as what you want to replace. Flooring, wall tile and a shower update can form a focused scope; changes to the layout, vanity or fixtures introduce additional decisions. Reference photos can explain a style preference without fixing the entire specification.

For a project centered on the shower, review shower remodeling. For a surface update, see tile installation.

Make responsibilities explicit

Before accepting an estimate, confirm who supplies each material and fixture, which work is included and what remains outside the scope. Any plumbing, electrical, structural or permit-related work should have its responsibilities and requirements established before scheduling.

If other contractors are involved, explain their scope and timing. Coordination should be discussed rather than assumed to be included.

Plan for bathroom access and concealed conditions

Tell us whether this is the only bathroom available and how much disruption you can accommodate. Explain known leaks, damaged surfaces or previous repairs. Hidden conditions may become clearer after removal, so ask how additional findings and changes to the agreed scope will be handled.

The sequence and schedule depend on the actual scope, material availability and any required coordination. No fixed completion time is promised from an online description.

Prepare your estimate request

Include your city, photos of the room, approximate dimensions, the items you want changed and any materials you have selected. A target budget is optional but can help explain your priorities. Note your desired timeframe and whether you are still exploring options or ready for a detailed project conversation.

Use the estimate form to describe the renovation in your own words. If flooring in nearby rooms is also changing, include it or review flooring replacement.

Project questions

Can I request a smaller bathroom refresh?

Describe the specific changes you want, even if the layout is staying the same. The estimate conversation should define those items and clarify any connected work.

Will an online request give me a final remodel price?

An online request starts the review. Existing conditions, dimensions, material choices and the included work need to be clarified before a project-specific estimate can be finalized.
