Flooring Replacement in Metro Atlanta

Replace the floor, or restore what you have?

If you have existing hardwood, refinishing may be worth discussing before deciding on replacement. If you want a different material, have areas that cannot be restored or need to change the layout, replacement may be the better direction. Photos help start the conversation, but the floor and substrate conditions determine the scope.

Compare hardwood refinishing with new hardwood installation. For a different flooring system, review engineered hardwood or LVP and laminate.

What belongs in a replacement estimate?

Identify the rooms and approximate area, existing floor layers, desired product and any stairs. Ask which removal, disposal, subfloor repairs, preparation, transitions and trim are included. Material purchase, furniture movement and appliance handling should be clarified rather than assumed.

Floor preparation is a separate part of planning the installation. Conditions revealed after removal may require a revised scope; discuss how those decisions will be handled before work begins.

Plan around access to your home

If you will be living in the home during the work, explain which rooms and entrances must remain accessible. Note furniture, pets, working-from-home needs and whether the project can be divided into phases. These details help establish a realistic sequence; they are not a promise that every floor can be completed in stages.

Details that help us review your project

Send your city, the rooms involved, approximate square footage and the material you are considering. Include photos of the current floor and transitions, plus your preferred timeframe. If you have already selected a product, share its name or specifications so installation requirements can be discussed.

Project questions

Can I get pricing from square footage alone?

Square footage is a useful starting point. Removal, subfloor condition, material, stairs and transitions can change the work required, so the estimate needs more than an area measurement.

Do I need to buy flooring first?

You can request an estimate while comparing materials. If you have already purchased flooring, include the product information and quantity so the scope can be reviewed.
