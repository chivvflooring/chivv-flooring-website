Planning Stair Treads, Transitions & Trim

Every flooring project has different conditions. We review the actual material, rooms, transitions and preparation needs so the estimate reflects the project.

List the stair and finish details explicitly

Photos of the full staircase, landings and connection to adjoining floors help explain the request. Identify the treads, risers, nosing and trim you want reviewed. If the surrounding floor is staying, mention the intended relationship between its finish and the proposed work.

Clarify painting, staining and any work handled by another contractor before agreeing to the scope. For related room work, review hardwood installation or refinishing. Do not assume every railing or structural change is included in a flooring estimate.

How the project scope is reviewed

01 Project Review

Rooms, existing flooring and site conditions.

We review the staircase, tread/return configuration, risers, skirts, nosing, railing conditions, trim and how the stair flooring connects to adjacent floors.

02 Clear Scope

Project-specific preparation and installation details.

We define which treads, risers, skirts, nosing, shoe molding or trim items are included and whether painting or staining is part of the scope.

03 Professional Work

Complete the agreed flooring scope with attention to details.

The stair and trim work is completed to match the agreed flooring scope, with attention to alignment, transitions and finish details.
