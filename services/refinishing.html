Planning Your Hardwood Sanding & Refinishing

Every flooring project has different conditions. We review the actual material, rooms, transitions and preparation needs so the estimate reflects the project.

Decide what you want to keep and change

Send photos of the existing wood, worn areas, stains and previous repairs. Describe whether you want a natural appearance or a different finish direction. Existing material and condition affect the options, and a photo alone cannot establish whether every area can be restored.

Confirm the rooms, repairs, stairs and trim included in the proposed work, along with access during sanding and finishing. If you are also considering a new material, compare replacement and discuss the installation scope separately.

How the project scope is reviewed

01 Project Review

Rooms, existing flooring and site conditions.

We review the existing hardwood condition, damaged boards, old coatings, stains, repairs, stairs and areas that may need special attention.

02 Clear Scope

Project-specific preparation and installation details.

We confirm the sanding/refinishing scope, stain or natural finish direction, finish system, repairs, shoe molding and excluded areas.

03 Professional Work

Complete the agreed flooring scope with attention to details.

The floor is sanded through the proper sequence, edge/detail work is completed, and the selected stain, sealer or finish system is applied.
