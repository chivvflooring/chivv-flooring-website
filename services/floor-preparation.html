Preparing Your Subfloor for New Flooring

Every flooring project has different conditions. We review the actual material, rooms, transitions and preparation needs so the estimate reflects the project.

Describe what is visible and what is unknown

List existing coverings, known repairs and areas that appear uneven or damaged. If you do not know the subfloor material, say so. A preparation scope depends on the selected flooring system and the actual substrate, including conditions that may only be visible after removal.

Ask how any further findings will be reviewed before additional work is agreed. Connect preparation with the intended finish: engineered hardwood, hardwood or a wider flooring replacement project.

How the project scope is reviewed

01 Project Review

Rooms, existing flooring and site conditions.

We review the existing flooring, substrate, high and low areas, adhesive residue, damaged sections, moisture concerns and access conditions.

02 Clear Scope

Project-specific preparation and installation details.

We define exactly which removal, grinding, patching, leveling, repair or preparation items are included before new flooring begins.

03 Professional Work

Complete the agreed flooring scope with attention to details.

Preparation is completed before installation so the new flooring system has suitable substrate conditions for the selected material.
