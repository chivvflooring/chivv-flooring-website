## Production rules

Use vertical 9:16 for Reels/Stories, 4:5 for feed images and readable captions burned in only when accurate. Capture wide, medium, detail and 5–10 second stable clips. Keep house numbers, faces, paperwork, vehicle plates, access codes and private addresses out. Record permission, broad city, service, material, method and scope in an internal caption sheet. Suggested UTM pattern: `utm_source=instagram|facebook&utm_medium=organic_social&utm_campaign=content_day_##`; never put PII in URLs.
