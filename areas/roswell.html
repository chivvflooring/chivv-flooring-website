Decide how much of the existing floor to keep

Identify the areas that drive the decision

Photograph visible wear, damaged boards and places where different floors meet. Explain whether your concern is appearance, a known damaged area or the material itself. A picture starts the review but does not establish the condition beneath the surface.

Discuss matching without assuming a perfect match

When new flooring meets existing hardwood, material, dimensions and finish can affect the result. Tell us what you know about the existing product and whether spare boards are available. The intended transition should be discussed before the scope is agreed.

Include the finish details

Stair edges, shoe molding and doorway transitions are easy to overlook. List them with the rooms so the estimate can explain what is included and what is staying as it is.
