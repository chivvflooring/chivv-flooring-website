Common Project Questions

Can removal and preparation be included?
When needed and included in the agreed scope, a replacement estimate can address removal and preparation before installation.
What helps CHIVV understand the project?
City or ZIP, room list, approximate size, existing material, preferred new material, timing and optional photos are the best starting details.
Can flooring and stair details be discussed together?
Yes. Include stair treads, landings, trim and transitions in the request so those connections can be considered with the floor scope.
