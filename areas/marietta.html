Bring room, stair and trim decisions into one plan

Show the connections

Include photos of stair approaches, landings and doorways as well as the room floors. Note which surfaces should stay. If an existing stair finish needs to relate to a new floor, explain the intended result instead of assuming an exact match.

Define removal and preparation

Replacing flooring can involve different work from refinishing what is there. List known damage and uneven areas and ask how removal, repairs and preparation will be included. Concealed conditions may require a further scope discussion.

Include a bathroom update when relevant

If bathroom tile or a shower is part of the same renovation, list that work separately. It helps distinguish flooring installation from the additional decisions involved in a wet-area or room remodel.
