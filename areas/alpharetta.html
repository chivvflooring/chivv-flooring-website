Compare restoring your hardwood with replacing the floor

Work from the floor you have

Send photos of the current surface, worn areas and transitions into other rooms. Note known repairs and whether the same floor continues under furniture or into adjoining spaces. Those details help frame the choice between keeping the floor and starting over.

Compare complete scopes

A refinishing estimate and a replacement estimate cover different work. Ask about preparation, repairs, removal where applicable, trim and rooms excluded from the scope. Comparing only a price per square foot can hide those differences.

Coordinate adjoining spaces

If stairs, an entry or a bathroom floor are part of the plan, identify them separately. A defined list of rooms helps avoid unclear transitions and makes it easier to discuss which work should happen together.
