Organize a flooring or remodeling scope across the home

Separate the work by room

A whole-home request may combine rooms with different existing materials. Identify the surfaces being replaced, the hardwood being retained and any bathroom or shower work. This helps distinguish related tasks without treating them as identical installations.

Explain access and coordination

Share any actual property access rules, material delivery constraints and other contractors involved. If the home is occupied, identify the rooms and entrances needed during the work. Do not assume access or trade coordination is automatically included.

Use the most relevant service page

Review the service that matches your main project and add connected work to the estimate request. If the project is in Buckhead or Sandy Springs, the linked area pages include additional planning questions; you do not need to submit separate forms for each room.
