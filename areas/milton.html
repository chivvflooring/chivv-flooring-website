Coordinate flooring with the wider renovation

Explain the sequence

Share whether demolition, painting, cabinets or other work is planned in the same rooms. Include relevant access constraints and the dates you are working toward. A requested date is a planning input, not a confirmed completion promise.

Provide product information early

If a flooring product has been selected, include its name and specifications. Engineered and solid hardwood can have different requirements; preparation, layout and installation details should be reviewed for that product and substrate.

Separate floor work from connected trades

Identify who supplies materials and handles furniture, appliances or other renovation tasks. The estimate should name the flooring work and associated preparation rather than assume everything in the room is included.
